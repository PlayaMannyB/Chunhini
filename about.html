About Me

Hi, I'm Chunhini! 🌼 I'm a 24-year-old artist from the Philippines who loves creating warm, soft, and expressive artwork. I focus mainly on digital art, traditional graphite sketches, and photography, anything that lets me capture emotions, little moments, and stories that feel personal and comforting.

I'm inspired by the people around me, and giving life to characters with their own moods, aesthetics, and small details that make them feel real.

My style leans toward gentle lines, soft palettes, and emotion-driven expressions. I love creating pieces that feel cozy, the kind of art that reminds you of quiet mornings, warm hugs, cats purring on your lap, and the soft comfort of being understood.

Speaking of cats… yes, I'm obsessed. 😸💕 Cute animals in general are my weakness, but cats have a special place in my heart. They inspire a lot of my art and photography because they're effortlessly adorable, chaotic, and perfect.

Outside of drawing, I enjoy taking aesthetic photos, from moody lighting to candid cozy moments and the little details most people overlook. Photography helps me slow down, breathe, and find inspiration in everyday scenes: sunsets, sketchbooks, quiet corners, iced coffee, soft mornings, and tiny joys.

I'm the kind of person who finds comfort in music, late-night doodles, soft vibes, and supporting fellow creatives. I love meeting people who enjoy art, storytelling, and wholesome aesthetics.

If you're into OCs, aesthetic art, slice-of-life vibes, cute animals, or you just want a friendly moot, feel free to message me anytime! 🌸💌 Let's share inspo, support each other, and build a cozy creative space together.

Thank you for visiting my little corner of the internet~ uwu 💕✨
